\section{Issues Discussed \& Solutions Agreed}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Scope control:} Avoid over-building complex systems such as a complete shop or monetisation system during the initial iterations.
    \item \textbf{Core game loop:} Begin with a simple, reliable loop containing towers and waves, supported by a basic list of available modules.
    \item \textbf{Player story:} Focus on the complete player journey: how users discover, adopt, and evaluate new modules over time.
    \item \textbf{Early evaluation:} Use low-fidelity methods such as wireframes, paper storyboards, and targeted questionnaires before committing to heavy coding.
    \item \textbf{Event logging:} Design structured logging so that the resulting data can be queried in spreadsheets or SQL to evaluate the research questions.
\end{itemize}
