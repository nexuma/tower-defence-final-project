# Issues Discussed & Solutions Agreed

- **Scope control:** Avoid over-building complex systems such as a complete shop or monetisation system during the initial iterations.

- **Core game loop:** Begin with a simple, reliable loop containing towers and waves, supported by a basic list of available modules.

- **Player story:** Focus on the complete player journey: how users discover, adopt, and evaluate new modules over time.

- **Early evaluation:** Use low-fidelity methods such as wireframes, paper storyboards, and targeted questionnaires before committing to heavy coding.

- **Event logging:** Design structured logging so that the resulting data can be queried in spreadsheets or SQL to evaluate the research questions.
